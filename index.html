Memory Lane — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Memory Lane is a memory-training game that uses photos you choose. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your data stays on your device, and we do not collect it.

Information we collect

None. Memory Lane does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Photos and camera

With your permission, the app can access your photo library or camera so you can add photos to albums for the memory games. These photos are stored only on your device, inside the app. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party. You can remove photos from an album at any time, and deleting the app removes all of its data.

Game progress and settings

Your albums, game results and preferences are saved locally on your device so the app can remember them. This information is not sent anywhere.

Third parties

Memory Lane does not use third-party services that receive your data.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
